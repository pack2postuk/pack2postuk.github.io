How it works

Simple, from your stock to your customer

You keep your own stock in your own storage. We come in, pack your orders and post them.

01

Stock arrives

When your stock is delivered to your storage, we handle the loading and unloading for you.

02

You send labels

Just provide us with the postage labels for your orders. That is all we need from you.

03

We pack

We pack every order carefully. We can also arrange the packaging material if you need it.

04

We dispatch

Labels go on, and your parcels are posted and on their way to your customers.
